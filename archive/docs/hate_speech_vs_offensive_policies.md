# Hate Speech vs. Offensive Content Policies

## Google's Policy
**Hate Speech**
Google defines hate speech as content that promotes or condones violence, discrimination, or disparagement, or aims to incite hatred against a group or individual based on protected characteristics such as race, religion, disability, age, nationality, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, caste, or other characteristics associated with systemic discrimination. Hate speech often includes calls to action that create danger or advocate violence against a specific group.

**Offensive Content**
Offensive content is a more general term for material likely to cause anger, insult, or disrespect, but it may not qualify as hate speech. This can include profanity, graphic imagery, or other shocking material. While offensive content is also prohibited in certain contexts by Google, the main difference from hate speech is the intent to incite hatred, discrimination, or violence based on a protected group. Impolite or rude content that doesn't target a protected group for harm is generally considered offensive rather than hate speech.

## Facebook's Distinction
**Hate Speech (Prohibited)**
A direct attack that dehumanizes, promotes hatred, or calls for exclusion/violence against individuals or groups based on their identity. It is intended to vilify, humiliate, or incite hatred, and is considered harmful to both the targeted individuals and society at large. Targets people based on specific protected characteristics.

**Offensive Speech (General)**
Subjective language that can be considered rude, crude, or in poor taste (e.g., certain jokes, profanity). May be hurtful, but does not necessarily aim to incite hatred, discrimination, or violence. Can be general or personal insults not tied to protected characteristics.

## Key Takeaway
The critical difference is that all hate speech is offensive, but not all offensive speech is hate speech. Policies focus on removing content that poses a risk of harm or promotes discrimination and violence, rather than simply content that some users might find insulting or disagreeable.
